## How the sources fit together

- **Revenue FY2023–FY2025 and the EUR 100M target** come from one company press release (March 2026), reproduced by Bursa.ro (S01) and reported by Ziarul Financiar (S02).
- **The RON 500M+ target** comes from S06; the **H1 2026 growth (+2%)** and the 10–12% growth objective from S05.
- **Average bill:** FY2023 EUR 50 (S09), H1 2024 ~EUR 45 (S10), FY2025 RON 290 (S02). Definitions differ between years.
- **Acquisitions:** Marty Restaurants majority stake, Oct 2024 (S17); Aubergine 49% stake c. 2021 (S08 headline).
- **Exchange rates:** BNR annual averages for 2024 and 2025 from S30; 2023 cross-checked in S53; S54 is the official BNR site.
- **S39** holds third-party EUR conversions that use the wrong-period exchange rate. They are kept for transparency and not used.
- **S52** is the company website, used only for the logo and brand colours.
